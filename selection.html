Step-by-Step Guide

Simple 4-Step Selection Process

Follow these visual steps to submit your final selection in clean sequence.

01

Open Your Gallery Link

Click the private gallery link sent by Rhythm Clicks Studio on your WhatsApp or Email. You can open it on your phone or computer.

  • Browse albums (e.g. Newborn, Maternity, Family, Milestones).
  • Click on any photo you like to view it.
02

Tap Info (ⓘ) & Note the 'Filename'

Tap the 'ⓘ Info' icon to open the Details panel. Look strictly at the Filename line (e.g. _CS_0548.jpg).

  • ⚠️ We ONLY need the Filename (e.g. _CS_0548.jpg).
  • You do not need dimensions, camera, or file size.
03

Add Numbers to Our Tool

Type or paste the filenames into our online tool below. It automatically numbers them in sequence (1, 2, 3...) and prevents duplicate entries.

  • Enter single numbers or paste comma-separated lists.
  • Auto-arranges in clean sequence for the editing team.
04

Send via WhatsApp or Copy

Click 'Share with WhatsApp' or 'Copy Formatted List'. Rhythm Clicks Studio will confirm receipt and begin editing!

  • Instant confirmation message from our studio.
  • Professional retouching begins right away.
