Welcome to Our Studio

Where Every Precious Click Tells a Story

Based in Gandhinagar and serving both Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar, Rhythm Clicks Studio was founded in 2018 as a collaborative dream by three passionate photographers: Priya Patel, Purva Patel, and Bijal Patel. Together, we specialize in the delicate art of maternity, newborn, toddler, kids, and family photography.

As a dedicated three-member team, we combine our unique artistic visions and technical strengths to create a seamless, comfortable, and personalized experience. Our custom-designed studio is a warm, cozy, and completely safe environment equipped with an extensive, curated selection of boutique props, organic knit wraps, and elegant designer gowns imported especially for our sessions.

Exclusive Fine Art

Signature Master Edits

Our exclusive range of hand-crafted Master Edits transforms everyday portraits into timeless, painterly masterpieces.

Every Master Edit is individually edited pixel-by-pixel, applying advanced color grading, painterly textures, and artistic lighting composites. We spend hours on each image to bring a magical, fantasy-like mood that is perfect for newborn crescent moon setups, ethereal maternity silhouettes, and whimsical toddler milestones.

Note: These exclusive fine-art Master Edits are included directly within our premium session packages. They are not offered as a standalone session, ensuring you receive the highest level of finished art as part of your booking.
