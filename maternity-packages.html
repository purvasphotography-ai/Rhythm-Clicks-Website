Studio Policies

Shoot Details & Guidelines

Clear policies to ensure a smooth, professional, and relaxed experience for your special day.

Booking & Deposit Terms

To officially lock your date and slot, a 50% non-refundable advance deposit is required. The remaining 50% balance must be settled on the day of the photoshoot immediately after the session concludes. No booking is confirmed without the advance payment.

Wardrobe & Personal Prep

Curated gown styling is included in Packages 2, 3, 5, and 6. Clients are requested to bring their own matching basic undergarments (like skin-colored seamless cups or strapless bras). Hair and makeup should be pre-arranged unless you request a professional artist add-on through our studio.

Photo Selection & Delivery Timelines

We believe in delivering perfection. Unedited raw files are the copyright of the studio and are not shared. A selection gallery link containing proof sheets will be sent within 5 days of your shoot. The fully retouched, high-resolution soft copies will be delivered within 25 to 30 days of your final selections. Printed photobooks take an additional 10-15 days for layout printing and shipping.

Rescheduling & Cancellations

Maternity timelines can sometimes be unpredictable. If you need to reschedule due to health concerns or severe weather warnings, please inform us at least 48 hours in advance. We will accommodate one reschedule without penalties, subject to available slots in our calendar.

Image Copyright & Promotions

Rhythm Clicks Studio retains the absolute copyright of all images captured. We reserve the right to share selected photos on our digital portfolio and social media handles for advertising purposes. If you wish to keep your shoot completely private, a privacy charge equal to 20% of the package cost will apply at the time of booking.

Ready to reserve your date?

Slots are limited. We recommend booking your session when you are in your 28th to 34th week of pregnancy.
