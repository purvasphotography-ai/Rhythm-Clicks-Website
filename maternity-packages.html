Studio Policies

Shoot Details & Guidelines

Please read our guidelines carefully. By paying the booking amount, you agree to all the terms below.

Booking & Deposit Terms

• Once a package is finalized, you must secure your photoshoot session by paying a non-refundable advance deposit of ₹2,000 (accepting online payments or studio visits).
• The remaining balance is to be paid on the day of the shoot at the studio/location.
• Your session can be rescheduled only once for up to one month based on availability, provided we receive notification at least 2 days prior.
• Current packages and rates are applicable at the time of booking. Rates from past inquiries may differ.

Deliverables Cycle & Timelines

• RAW/unedited images are sent via a WhatsApp link for selection purposes only. RAW video files are not provided.
• Fully edited photos will be delivered within 20 days of your final selection date.
• You must share your selections with us within 30 days of the shoot. If not shared, we won't be liable to edit the images.
• Premium photobooks are delivered within 30 days of your confirmation of edited photos. Pickup is in person from our studio, or home delivery can be arranged for an additional fee.
• Rhythm Clicks Studio holds data storage for two months only after the session date.
• Photobooks must be picked up within 15 days of notification. We are not responsible for any damage/mishandling if left beyond this frame.

General Studio Instructions

• Reserve your slot 15 days in advance for weekdays and 1 month in advance for weekends.
• Arrive strictly on time. If late, the session will be completed only within the remaining duration of your package (time slots cannot be extended as we have other shoots scheduled).
• Only one additional person is permitted to accompany you as assistance during the session.
• Photos with other relatives or friends will be charged an additional ₹500 per person (includes 1 extra edit).
• If you wish to wear footwear during the session, it must be clean from the bottom and top, packed in a bag, and brought with you.
• Digital copyrights stay with Rhythm Clicks Studio.

Maternity Session Guidelines

• **Timing:** 28 to 34 weeks of pregnancy is the ideal window for the photoshoot.
• **Makeup:** Packages do not include makeup; clients should prepare beforehand and show up ready.
• **Gown Choice:** Gowns must be chosen from the link provided and informed to us 1 day before the session. Trials on the shoot day are not permitted.
• **Costumes:** The number of costumes remains the same whether using your own costumes or ours. Drapes and white gowns are not offered for outdoor sessions.
• **Backgrounds:** "1 background" refers to a plain paper or textured background chosen by our styling team based on your dress.
• **Outdoors:** We prefer early morning sessions for outdoor shoots. If bad weather prevents outdoor shooting, sessions will continue in the studio.

Planning a Newborn Shoot? (Guidelines)

• **Ideal Age:** The best period for newborn photography is between 5 and 15 days for open and curly poses. Shoots can be done up to two months.
• **Feeding & Breaks:** Brief breaks are allowed for feeding and soothing the baby, but shoots cannot be postponed if the baby refuses to cooperate. We must complete within the allotted time.
• **Themes:** Choose themes 1 day prior from the link provided. 1 theme includes: 1 wrap/outfit, 1 prop, and 1 background.
• **Twins:** Twin packages will be charged as Package × 1.5.
• **Personal Prep:** Please bring baby food, wet wipes, diapers, and toys to keep the baby comfortable. Select a shoot slot based on the baby's usual mood.

Ready to reserve your date?

Slots are limited. We recommend booking your session when you are in your 28th to 34th week of pregnancy.
